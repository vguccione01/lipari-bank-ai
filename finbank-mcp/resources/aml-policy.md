# LipariBank Anti-Money Laundering Policy

**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Compliance Department  
**Review cycle:** Annual, or after a material regulatory change

## Purpose

This policy defines the minimum controls used by LipariBank to prevent money laundering, terrorist financing, and sanctions violations.

## Core controls

- Apply a risk-based customer due diligence process before account activation.
- Verify customer identity and identify beneficial owners for legal entities.
- Collect information about the expected purpose and normal activity of the relationship.
- Monitor transactions for unusual patterns, rapid movement of funds, structuring, and activity inconsistent with the customer profile.
- Screen customers and transactions against applicable sanctions and watch lists.
- Escalate suspicious activity to the Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO).

## Alert handling

Alerts must be documented, assigned to a trained analyst, and resolved with a clear rationale. Where suspicion remains after review, the case must be escalated according to the bank's suspicious transaction reporting procedure. Staff must not disclose an investigation or report to the customer.

## Records and training

Customer due diligence, transaction-monitoring decisions, and investigation records must be retained for the period required by applicable law. Employees involved in onboarding, payments, or investigations receive periodic AML training.
